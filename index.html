Who we are

Local action. Shared progress.

The Isaac Koskei Kimutai Foundation is a community-focused initiative dedicated to helping the people of Kuresoi North build safer, healthier, better educated, and more resilient communities. We believe lasting development grows where people work together, resolve differences peacefully, and have fair access to opportunity.

Our mission

To empower the people of Kuresoi North through peacebuilding, education, health interventions, community development, conflict resolution, leadership development, and strategic partnerships that promote social cohesion, security, and sustainable livelihoods for all.

Our vision

A peaceful, united, healthy, educated, and prosperous Kuresoi North where everyone has the opportunity and support to realize their potential and contribute to sustainable community development.

“Peace is not only the absence of conflict; it is the foundation on which communities learn, thrive, and prosper.”

We bring people together to identify local priorities, strengthen community capacity, and turn shared ideas into practical action.

Practical programs for community empowerment

We aim to work alongside residents, community groups, professionals, public institutions, and development partners to respond to local needs.

Peace, Security & Conflict Resolution

Promoting peaceful coexistence, early response to tensions, and community-led approaches to safety.

  • Community dialogue and security forums
  • Mediation and reconciliation meetings
  • Peace education and conflict prevention
02

Education & Scholarships

Helping learners access education and make informed choices about their futures.

  • Scholarships and education support
  • Mentorship and career guidance
  • School retention and learning support
03

Health & Medical Outreach

Supporting community access to health information, preventive services, and outreach.

  • Medical camps with qualified providers
  • Health awareness and prevention
  • Referrals and support for vulnerable families
04

Youth & Women Empowerment

Building practical skills, confidence, leadership, and pathways to economic participation.

  • Skills and entrepreneurship training
  • Leadership and life-skills development
  • Mentorship and group initiatives
05

Support for Vulnerable Families

Working to ensure that people facing hardship are treated with dignity and included in community life.

Livelihoods & Community Development

Encouraging locally driven solutions that improve resilience and quality of life.

How we work

Values that guide our service

Our approach is grounded in dignity, community participation, responsible stewardship, and measurable results.

Peace & Reconciliation

We choose dialogue, mediation, and cooperation over violence and division.

Integrity

We commit to transparent decisions and responsible use of resources.

Inclusion

We value participation across age, gender, ability, background, and circumstance.

Community Ownership

We listen to residents and support locally identified priorities.

Accountability

We learn from feedback and report on activities and results.

Respect & Dignity

We uphold human dignity and respect the rights of every person.

Partnership

We collaborate to combine expertise, resources, and local knowledge.

Sustainability

We seek solutions that communities can maintain and build on over time.
